Backward Compatibility

The Xbox Series X features backward compatibility for all of the previous Xbox consoles: Xbox, Xbox 360, and Xbox one. Very few games are not availible trhough Xbox's backward compatibility program.

Quick Resume

The Xbox Series X has an all new unqiue feature called quick resume. This lets users to pause multiple games and resume them right where they left off, allowing them to switch between games instantly and even resume after turning their console off.

Performance & Storage

The Xbox Series X is a powerful console, featuring a custom AMD processor and GPU capable of true 4K gaming up to 120 FPS. It also includes a fast 1TB custom SSD, which significantly reduces load times and improves overall system performance. Additional storage can also be added using expansion cards.

Game Pass

Xbox Game Pass is a subscription service that gives players access to online multiplayer and a large library of games, including new releases from Xbox Game Studios on day one. This allows users to explore many titles without purchasing them individually. Xbox Game Pass has 3 subscription tiers currently priced at $9.99, $14.99, and $29.99 per month

Review

The Xbox Series X is an amazing choice for price and convenience. If you have a huge stash of games you built up over the years from previous consoles, or you're looking to build a digital library, seeing that games on the xbox store are constantly having huge sales, this could be the console for you.
