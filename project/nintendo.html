Overview

Capabilities: 1080p (4k Docked), 120 FPS, HDR, Raytracing

Features: Handheld and Docked, 2 Joycons, and Backward Compatibility

Storage: 256 GB

Dimensions: 10.7in x 4.5in x .55in

Weight: 1.18 lbs

Price: $449.99

Link: https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch-2/

Backward Compatibility

The Nintendo Switch 2 supports backward compatibility with a majority of switch 1 games. There are even starting to bring back classics from previous consoles, although you will have to buy them again at a high price.

Hybrid Design

The Nintendo Switch 2 continues the unique hybrid design, allowing users to play games both on a TV and as a handheld device. This flexibility makes it easy to switch between home and portable gaming. Each joycon can also be used seperate as their own controller, allowing for a coop expericnce without buying another set.

Performance & Storage

The Nintendo Switch 2 improves performance over the original Switch with upgraded hardware allowing 1080p handheld and 4k resolution while docked. It also includes internal storage with the option to expand using microSD cards.

Nintendo Switch Online

Nintendo Switch Online is a subscription service that provides access to online multiplayer and a collection of classic games. It also includes cloud saves and additional features for supported games, with pricing starting at $3.99 per month.

Review

The Nintendo switch 2 is best fit for those who love to game on the go or to enjoy with the family. It provides the classic feel of gaming that you might remember from being a kid, With classics like mario and Zelda.
